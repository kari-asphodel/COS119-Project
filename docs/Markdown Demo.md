### For Bold and Italic Text:

Bold: **This text is bold** use two ** ** between the text you want bold

Italics: *This text is italicized* use a single * * between your text you want italicized

Bold + Italic: ***This text is bold and italicized*** use 3 *** *** between your text you want bold and italicized
